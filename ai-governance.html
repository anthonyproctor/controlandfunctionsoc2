Questions we get

Answers before you have to ask.

Is this an AI audit or a certification?

No. We are not an audit firm. We build the controls, documentation and evidence so that a customer review, an auditor, or your own committee gets a straight answer quickly.

Which frameworks do you map to?

Primarily the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001, plus your existing SOC 2 scope wherever AI systems now touch controls you already attest to.

We already have SOC 2. Is this a separate program?

Mostly not. If you have a working control environment, AI governance is a scoping, documentation and monitoring extension of it. Starting from an existing program is much cheaper than starting cold.

Do we need this if we only use third party AI tools?

Often yes, and that case is usually worse, because the tools were adopted without review and nobody has an inventory. Third party use does not move accountability off you in your customer's eyes.
